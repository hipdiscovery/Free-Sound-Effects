# Free assets for CapCut & short-form creators

Reviewed 2026-10-25. This is a carefully filtered **link directory**: the repository does not rehost stock videos, music, sound packs, icons, illustration packs, or individual images. A published landing page and a platform-wide license do **not** guarantee that every third-party upload is cleared. Verify the specific clip, trademark, releases, license, and music claim status before distribution. Links can change.

## Practical use rules

1. Use **CC0-first** for motion sprites, textures, digital/game sounds and overlays when possible; keep source/creator evidence.
2. For Mixkit footage, check the **specific clip** says *Stock Video Free License*. Mixkit also has a **Restricted License** and promoted paid results; free visibility alone is not sufficient.
3. For Pexels, Unsplash, Pixabay, Coverr and unDraw, usage in original finished video edits is allowed by platform terms, but not independent repackaging as a stock library. Screen logos, brand marks, depicted artworks and recognizable people may need separate rights.
4. For Freesound, filter by **Creative Commons Zero**. Many sounds are CC BY or CC BY-NC, which are **not equivalent** to CC0. Uploader provenance still needs judgment.
5. For Lucide, Phosphor and Tabler SVG source files, keep their relevant ISC/MIT notices if distributing icon source files. Export individual assets to PNG for CapCut; do not assume desktop imports SVG directly.
6. Check music **platform scope and Content ID** separately. Claims can occur even when the music license permits use. Keep download dates, exact track names, and licenses. For cross-posted clips, avoid assuming YouTube's Audio Library or CapCut Commercial Sounds cover TikTok, Twitch and YouTube alike.
7. A website may offer premium results alongside free ones. Select the *verified free* source/item, not a sponsored upsell.

## What was researched but intentionally excluded

- **CapCut built-in 'Commercial Sounds' as cross-platform music**: [CapCut's Materials License](https://www.capcut.com/clause/material-license-agreement) confines many commercial sounds to permitted CapCut/TikTok surfaces unless separate rights are obtained. Ordinary 'Sounds' may be personal-only.
- **YouTube Audio Library as a universal cross-platform clearance**: [YouTube's own help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3376882) confirms YouTube copyright safety, but explicitly does not provide off-platform legal guidance. Safe on YouTube does not by itself mean safe everywhere.
- **Pixabay background music as a default zero-claim recommendation**: [Pixabay FAQ](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/) says some legally licensed music is Content ID registered. It may still be useful after track-by-track checking, but not a frictionless default.
- **Mixkit editor-project transition templates**: [Mixkit's templates](https://mixkit.co/free-video-templates/) target Premiere Pro, After Effects, Final Cut and DaVinci Resolve, not native CapCut projects. Use MP4 stock clips/overlays instead.
- **BBC, game/meme audio rips, Discord/iPhone notification samples and unnamed TikTok 'no copyright' packs**: provenance, platform scope, trademark, or commercial rights are not sufficiently clear for this library.
- **Generic paid-tier 'free' packs and sources requiring hidden trials or recurring subscriptions**: excluded as low fit with a reusable no-spend asset directory.
