# Sources and license review

## Imported: Kenney Interface Sounds 1.0

- Original creator: [Kenney](https://kenney.nl).
- Original source: [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds).
- Checked: 2026-10-20. The official page labels this pack Creative Commons CC0 and lists 100 files. The official ZIP's bundled `License.txt` also dedicates this content under CC0 and permits personal, educational and commercial use.
- [CC0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) and [legal code](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode): copying, modification and redistribution, including commercial use, are allowed. Trademark, privacy, publicity and other third-party rights are separate. No creator endorsement is implied.
- Imported exactly the 100 unchanged `Audio/*.ogg` files. No Kenney branding or shortcut files were imported.
- Preserved notice: [kenney-interface.txt](../licenses/kenney-interface.txt). Structured review, original archive SHA-256 and page response hash: [kenney-interface-review.json](kenney-interface-review.json). Per-file integrity and measured Ogg metadata: [catalog.json](../catalog.json).
- Source names become categories; synonym tags aid discovery. Card bar motifs are decorative, not measured waveforms. Duration is measured from Vorbis sample rate and final Ogg granule position.

## Not imported: Muaaz social-post collection

Original lead: https://x.com/mws/status/2112338141965144309?s=46

The original page could not be read directly in the initial web fetch. X's public syndication endpoint returned the author's text on 2026-10-20, corroborated by a third-party FxTwitter response. Browser review then recovered the author's [reply](https://x.com/mws/status/2112338144687169679), linking to [Sound Effects Creators Actually Use on Notion](https://muaazws.notion.site/Sound-Effects-Creators-Actually-Use-3fe84fe99b3181ad9cbed1be16bfa69b). The Notion page contains audio previews and links to [the ZIP offer](https://muaaz.lol/products/sfx).

Reviewed both linked pages on 2026-10-20. They offer sounds for use in creator content, but neither displayed an explicit redistribution license or original-source license trail. The Notion page includes branded notification and entertainment names (for example Apple Pay, Discord, Pokemon and SpongeBob); these labels are unresolved provenance signals, not proof of infringement. The ZIP offer describes MP3 delivery by email and newsletter subscription. We did not subscribe, request email delivery, download or mirror these files. No audio or video from this collection was copied or embedded. Original ownership and permission to redistribute a public library remain unverified.

Free access is not evidence of permission to mirror a collection. A future contribution must identify each original source, retain license evidence, and verify redistribution rights before import. Do not label these 297 effects CC0 based on the post.

## Review limits

The automated validator checks recorded provenance fields, local license evidence, audio metadata, catalog coverage and exact-byte SHA-256 duplicates. It cannot establish legal ownership, authenticate a creator's entire chain of title, detect all re-encoded or perceptually similar sounds, or guarantee that an external source will retain the same terms. Review new sources and listen before accepting them. This repository intentionally starts with one verified collection rather than mirroring unverified downloads.
